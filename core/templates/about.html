About EuphoriaDJ

A focused storefront for discovering and shortlisting everyday technology.

Our approach

A clear catalog, not a crowded aisle.

EuphoriaDJ is built around a simple idea: make it easy to find a product, understand its price, and keep track of the items worth revisiting. The storefront uses the project’s existing Django account system, so favorites and carts belong to the person who created them.

Browse by category, search by name or description, narrow the price range, and sort the results. When you find something useful, save it to your private favorites list or add it to your cart.

Save a shortlist

Favorites are stored against your account and are never shared with other shoppers.

Keep a cart and order history

Your cart persists between visits and calculates line totals from the stored product price. Checkout keeps an order snapshot, and order history is visible only to your account.
