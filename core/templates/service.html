Store information

What the storefront supports today, and where to go next.

Catalog search and filters

Use the shop page to search product names and descriptions, filter by category and price, sort the results, and move through pages.

Favorites

Signed-in shoppers can add or remove products from a private favorites list. Duplicate entries are prevented by the database.

Cart and coupons

Add products in quantities from 1 to 5, apply one coupon, and review the subtotal, discount, shipping, tax, and final payable amount. Prices come from the database and totals use Decimal values.

Accounts

Registration validates required fields and passwords. A welcome email is attempted after the account is created; an email failure does not remove the account.

Protected actions

Cart and favorite changes use POST requests with CSRF protection. Visitors are redirected to the existing sign-in page and returned safely afterward.

Checkout and orders

Checkout supports saved delivery addresses, Cash on Delivery, and Razorpay online payments when gateway credentials are configured. Customers can view private order history and payment status after ordering.
