Service & care.

Everything you need to know about choosing, saving, buying and following your objects.

A considered storefront should feel effortless. Here is how the experience works, from first look to order history.

01

Discover

Search names and descriptions, filter by category and price, sort the results and move through pages. The shop is drawn from the live catalogue.

02

Save

Signed-in clients can add or remove objects from a private favorites list. Duplicate entries are prevented by the database.

03

Collect

Add objects in quantities from 1 to 5, apply one coupon, and review the subtotal, discount, shipping, tax and final payable amount.

04

Account

Registration validates required fields and passwords. A welcome email is attempted after your account is created; an email failure never removes it.

05

Protected

Bag and favorite changes use POST requests with CSRF protection. Visitors are redirected to sign in and returned safely afterwards.

06

Order

Checkout supports saved delivery addresses, Cash on Delivery and Razorpay when gateway credentials are configured. Payment verification happens on the server.

A note on payment

Secure, verified, transparent.

Cash on Delivery keeps payment pending until collection. For online payment, Razorpay opens a secure window; EuphoriaDJ marks an order paid only after a server-side signature check. An order can be retried safely if a payment window is closed.

Order totals are always recalculated on the server before an order is created, so what you review is what is recorded.
